# Known Issues

**Related Guides**: [Root Overview](../README.md) | [Project Roadmap](ROADMAP.md) | [Patch Notes](PATCH_NOTES.md) | [Operational Runbook](RUNBOOK.md)

This page lists current gameplay limitations in **SandalCore**, what happens in game, any available operator workaround, and the current status. For upcoming features still in development, see the [Project Roadmap](ROADMAP.md).

**Last updated:** 7 November 2026

---

## Current Known Issues

| Area | What Happens | Workaround | Status |
|:---|:---|:---|:---|
| **Quests** | Beginner quests play through to completion where their objective types are implemented (including *Welcome to Verra*, *Emberbound*, and *Hungry For More*). Hand-ins that request items by tag or rarity, map-embedded objects for Harbinger quests, the separate Quest bag, and the choose-one reward selection window are not yet implemented. | A Game Master can complete a quest using `questctl finish <character> <quest>`. | Open: map-embedded objects, Quest bag, and choose-one reward window. |
| **Quest Givers** | About 50 of the 309 quest givers in the design data currently stand in the open world and towns; givers spawned dynamically by events or quests, as well as item-started quests, are not yet placed. | None. | Open: event-spawned quest givers and item-started quests. |
| **Quest Map Markers** | Active objectives with fixed coordinates receive a map pin or area marker when started. Kill objectives defined only by gameplay tags, random-area spawns, moving NPC pins, and overhead NPC speech bubbles are not yet drawn. | Read the quest tracker text for location hints. | Open: speech bubbles and tag-based kill markers. |
| **Crates** | Construction and trade crates can be crafted at stations and carried on the character's back (reducing movement speed by half). Using **Drop Crate** moves the crate into the item bag rather than placing it on the ground for other players, and crates cannot yet be turned in at building sites or sold for node currency. | Grant crates or currency with `worldctl give-item` and `worldctl give-money`. | Planned: ground crate actors and building site turn-ins. |
| **Crafting and Artisan Ratings** | Artisan ratings (quality, speed, extra output, rarity, and XP) currently come from equipped artisan gear (shirt, pants, tool belt) and active scrolls or elixirs. Food, drinks, and guild perks do not yet modify artisan ratings. | Equip artisan gear and activate scrolls or elixirs before starting a crafting job. | Planned: food, drinks, and guild buffs. |
| **Consumables** | Scrolls and elixirs apply their stat and artisan buffs for their full duration (`300 s` to `10,800 s`), replacing any prior scroll or elixir. Food, drinks, potions, and salves do not yet apply effects when used, and active buffs clear on logout. | None. | Planned: food, drinks, potions, salves, and persistent buffs across logins. |
| **Glint and Shops** | Vendors and trade crate tables currently require the exact Glint denomination listed by a price rather than making change from a higher Glint tier. | Keep a stack of Dull Glint in your bag. | Planned: automatic change from higher Glint tiers. |
| **Embersprings and Death Window** | The death window does not list the first Emberspring a character discovered (such as the Aelan Divine Gate for a new character). | Clicking **Revive** returns you to the starting area Emberspring. | Open. |
| **Weapon Talents** | Weapon skill lines earn XP from creature kills and GM level scrolls, but not yet from quests or gathering. The level-10 and level-20 GM scrolls leave a weapon line just shy of levels 10 and 20 because the client's scroll XP values sit slightly below the curve threshold. | Set weapon line levels directly with `worldctl weapon-xp <character> <line> <level>`. | Open: weapon XP from quests and gathering. |
| **Combat Reactions** | Reactions triggered by your own hits and ability costs run from `content.db` data and have been verified end-to-end on the Bard class; other classes' reactions (`Fighter` Momentum, `Mage` Essences, `Tank` Courage) run from data and are still being tuned against retail captures. Reactive effects triggered when you are hit (such as reflect or vengeance) do not fire yet. | Pass `--no-reactions` (or `AOC_NO_REACTIONS=1`) to disable combat reactions if needed. | Open: tuning remaining class reactions. |
| **Ability Timing and Projectiles** | Cast bars, charged shots (*Lightning Strike*, *Resplendent Beam*), and projectile travel times match retail captures across 24 spells and ranged weapons. *Caltrops* (ground-targeted) and the *Frostbolt* secondary shard do not fire yet, and homing projectiles (*Discordance*, *Mend*, *Salvo*) travel in a straight line rather than curving at a limited turn rate. | None. | Open. |
| **Bard Melodies** | *Menacing* and *Cathartic* melodies spend up to 3 Resonance at a combo finisher to add damage and apply `Shocked` or `Wounded`. *Cheerful*, *Pensive*, and *Epic* ally melodies do not yet trigger heals, mana restoration, or cleanses at a finisher. | None. | Open: ally finisher melodies. |
| **Rogue Throw** | The Rogue's *Throw* shuriken bounces to up to two nearby enemies but deals `0` damage because its formula construct in `content.db` is not yet compiled by `statprog`. | None. | Open. |
| **Bandit and Goblin AI** | Creature families play `429` authentic attack animations and fire projectiles (`fire bolt`, `oozing bomb`). Goblin Assassins do not yet rally, Flame Shamans do not raise their fire shield, Bandit Raiders do not leap from afar, and nearby mobs do not yet link aggro as a pack when one mob notices a player. | None. | Open: pack aggro and remaining special attacks. |
| **Walls, Water, and Fall Damage** | Mobs can still path through some thin fences, walls, and shallow lake beds, and falling or standing in lava does not yet deal damage. | None. | Planned: fall damage, lava damage, and fence/water navmesh blockers. |
| **Riverlands Boundary Biomes** | `GetNodeBiome()` can return undefined on a few outer boundary cells at the edge of the Riverlands, preventing regional reagent drops on those edge spawns. | Harvest inside central Riverlands territories. | Open: boundary cell interpolation. |
| **Inventory Stack Merging** | Dragging one item stack onto another stack of the same item to merge them, or splitting a stack, is currently refused by the server. | Keep stacks in separate bag slots. | Planned: stack merging and splitting. |
| **Town Navigation** | The five major towns include walkable terrain platforms (`town_platforms.bin`) and building collision (`town_layouts.bin`). However, closed doors, steep high-step stairs, and thin awnings are not modelled in the static navmesh, and changing a town's stage at runtime keeps the initial building collision mesh until the navmesh is rebuilt. | Walk into towns along main streets or use the Web Admin Studio to teleport to street level. | Open: dynamic navmesh tile swapping on City Node stage change. |
| **NPC Outfits** | A small number of randomized female mercenary outfits reference armor pieces that have no mesh for that body type in the client data, leaving those equipment slots bare. | None. | Open: fallback outfit rules. |
| **Ranger Abilities** | *Scatter Shot* can fail line-of-sight checks across uneven stream banks or ledges, and *Air Strike* applies slows to mobs without rooting them. | Fire *Scatter Shot* from level ground. | Open. |
| **Mage Ball Lightning** | *Ball Lightning* flies level along the camera direction rather than following terrain slopes, continues ticking if the Mage dies before it expires, and is not yet replicated to other nearby players. | None. | Open. |
| **Summoner Pets** | Guardian, Hunter, and Mystic pets summon, follow, fight, obey stances, and execute their 10 abilities. Because hard crowd control is not yet applied to mobs, pet stuns/roots/knockbacks apply only their slow component, and targeted pet abilities can still be triggered when no enemy is targeted. | None. | Planned: mob crowd control and target validation on pet abilities. |
| **Gathering Speed and Persistence** | Gathering durations are slightly longer than on retail while gathering speed rating curves are being finalized, overflow items drop as an ash pile when the resource bag is full, and cut trees reset when the server restarts. | Keep free slots in your resource bag before harvesting. | Open: gathering speed rating application and persistent foliage state. |
| **Aquatic and Mayor Mounts** | Mounting an aquatic mount while already swimming in deep water fails to complete possession, and the 44 mayor-specific flying mounts fail client-side item validation unless the character is a node mayor. | Mount on the shoreline before entering deep water, and use the Eagle Gryphon mounts for flight testing. | Open: water-volume mount possession and mayoral election milestones. |
| **Missing Navmesh (`0 HP` Mobs)** | If `dist/content/area_nav.bin` is missing, `world` does not start its combat subsystem and mobs appear with `0 HP`. | Run `.\start.bat` or `.\sandal.ps1 extract`, which automatically extracts and compiles `content.db` (`v41`) and `area_nav.bin`. | Automatic during `start.bat` and `.\sandal.ps1 extract`. |
| **Mob Crowd Control** | Damage-over-time debuffs (`Burning`, `Bleeding`, `Hemorrhage`, `Poison`), stat debuffs (`Shaken`, `Volatile`, `Demoralized`, `Blinded`), and movement slows/snares (`MoveSpeed`, `Slow`, `Snare`, `Chilled`) work on mobs, but hard crowd control (stuns, roots, knockbacks, sleeps) does not yet halt mob movement. | None. | Open: hard crowd control on mobs. |
| **Multiplayer Observer Replication** | Players within `150 m` see each other's characters standing, walking, and crossing worker borders. Observer replication for mounted riders, live gear/level changes after login, live health/mana bars, death states, combat stances, and player-on-player abilities is still in progress. | Relog to refresh gear or level changes for other players. | In Progress: full observer state replication. |
